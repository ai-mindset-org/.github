We run educational labs and build a platform, products, a community and special projects. This is an environment for those who explore what AI makes possible and want to keep their authorship, their attention and their own rhythm.

`>25 labs` · `4 years of building labs` · `10+ directions and special projects` · `1600+ participants` · `30+ countries` · `>40 speakers across all cohorts`

### Lab > course

We do not teach, and we think it is pointless to teach anyone in the age of AI better than Claude Code does. We assemble a space where the technological transition is lived through rather than studied. Everyone works on their own material: their own tasks, their own data, their own stack – and assembles a system that stays with them after the lab.

That is why we have no permanent program: every cohort is rebuilt from scratch, for the group and for what AI has become by that month. Only the principles stay constant.

**context** · context engineering – the right environment matters more than the right prompt. Working with meaning is the core competence

**exoskeleton** · a partner, not a service – the power of AI is tamed and bounded, and then it extends what you already know how to do

**make** · with your hands, not with notes – a working system built by your own hands every week: your own assistants, your own processes

**slowing down** · two notes instead of a chord – we do less than the technology allows, and slower than is customary

### Made inside – by the team's own hands

We build what we use ourselves – and invite people inside. We measure our success by one sign: do people come back. They do.
